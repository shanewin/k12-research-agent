# Email Examples: Before and After

## Example 1: State Money + Literacy Gap (Email 1)

### BAD (labels subject, cites stats, lists features)
Subject: Improving Reading Proficiency in Your District

Hi Dr. Martinez,

Your district's ELA proficiency rate of 17.3% shows a critical need for
intervention. With $19.5M in LCFF supplemental funding, you have the resources
to address this gap. the product's AI reading tutor provides real-time fluency
feedback and Lexile progress monitoring that your current programs aren't
delivering. Districts using the product see measurable improvement in 90 days.
Can we schedule 15 minutes to discuss how the product fits your literacy goals?

### GOOD (teases subject, references their work, sounds human)
Subject: Your structured literacy push

Hi Dr. Martinez,

I noticed your board greenlit a structured literacy initiative this spring —
that's a direction a lot of Central Valley districts are moving toward.

We built the product as the daily practice layer that sits alongside those
programs: students read aloud, the AI listens, and teachers get fluency
data without extra prep.

Would it be worth a 15-minute look to see if it fits what you're building?

## Example 2: Mandated to Improve (Email 2 in sequence)

### BAD (says "following up", cites stats, tells them what to do)
Subject: Following Up — Evidence-Based Intervention for CSI Schools

Hi Mr. Chen,

Following up on my last email. Your district has 4 schools on the CSI list,
indicating systemic challenges with reading achievement. You're required to
implement evidence-based interventions. the product provides AI-guided reading
practice with Lexile progress data. Your School Improvement and Title I
funds can cover this.

### GOOD (fresh opener, asks a question, no "following up")
Subject: The post-screener question

Hi Mr. Chen,

Your board's March discussion about the improvement plan updates sounded
like there's real momentum on the reading intervention piece.

A few districts in similar spots have been using AI reading practice to
bridge the gap between screener results and daily instruction.

Is that something on your radar, or are you set on the current approach?

## Example 3: Email 3+ in sequence (short, direct, no preamble)

### BAD (starts with "one more thought", feature dump)
Subject: One More Thought — EL Reading Fluency

Hi Ms. Nguyen,

One more thought from me. With 2,400 EL students and 43% at Beginning
level on ELPAC, your district has a clear need for English reading fluency
support. the product's AI pronunciation feedback helps EL students build the
oral reading skills needed for reclassification. Title III and Title I
funds are designed for exactly this purpose.

### GOOD (jumps straight in, connects to something they're doing)
Subject: Your dual language expansion

Hi Ms. Nguyen,

I noticed your dual language program is expanding — districts running DL
alongside AI reading practice have found it helps bridge the fluency gap,
especially for students transitioning between languages.

Happy to share what we've seen if it's useful.
